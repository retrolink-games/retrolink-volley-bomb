# Credits

## Original work

Everything you see and hear in Retro Link Volley Bomb is original to this project, apart from the Retro Link brand font below:

- **Graphics** are generated by `tools/make_assets.py` from designs on the Retro Link website hero (`website/main.js` in the Retro Link repo, our own work): the 10×10 blob, the sand ball, the night scene (sky bands, stars, ridges, trees) and the teal/coral/sand palette. The P3 sand and P4 violet colourways and the bomb explosion were made for this game.
- **Sound effects and the win jingle** are written directly to the GBA's DMG sound registers in `src/sound/sfx.cpp`. No samples.
- **Name, rules and code** are original.

The game contains no sprites, fonts, music, sounds, names or likenesses from any commercial game.

## Font

Text uses **Silkscreen** by Jason Kottke, Retro Link's brand pixel font, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`docs/licenses/Silkscreen-OFL.txt`). `tools/make_assets.py` rasterises `tools/fonts/Silkscreen-{Regular,Bold}.ttf` on its native 8 px grid into the GBA sprite fonts. It is a free, openly licensed font, not a game asset.
